# Google Ads Transparency API

> Advertisers and their ads on Search, YouTube and Display.

Canonical page: https://okdata.online/apis/google/

2 endpoints, $0.004 to $0.004 per call. Region: Global. No Google Ads Transparency login or cookies needed.

| Endpoint | ID | Returns | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Google Ads Transparency advertiser search](https://okdata.online/apis/google/advertisers/search/) | `google.advertisers.search` | Advertiser[] | $0.004 per call · about 10 advertisers per page |
| [Google advertiser ads](https://okdata.online/apis/google/advertisers/ads/list/) | `google.advertisers.ads.list` | Ad[] | $0.004 per call · about 40 ads per page |

## Good for

- Ad intelligence: `google.advertisers.search`, `google.advertisers.ads.list`
- Competitor monitoring: `google.advertisers.ads.list`
